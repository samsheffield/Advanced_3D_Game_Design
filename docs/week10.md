## Level Planning & Blockouts 

__Level Layout & Planning__

You are responsible for developing sketches and plans of your levels outside of Unity. The levels should be designed around your core mechanics and reinforce your player experience goals. __This is not an optional step__.

_Deliverable:_

Collect your game's layout planning in a digital document and discuss with your group. We'll also take a look at them together next week in class. 

__Greybox/Blockout__

A first pass at blocked out/greyboxed version of your game. Do not focus on finalizing game assets this week.


_Deliverable:_

Unity or Unreal project with blocked out scene(s). Player should be able to move around and use basic mechanics in the spaces. We'll discuss these together next week.
