# Blue sky thinking and team formation!

## Schedule

### 9:00-11:30 - Blue sky thinking exercise
We will form teams based on shared interests this morning. The plan is to:
- Everyone contributes 1-2 vague ideas/concepts to a shared pool
- Add additional details to the pool of ideas
- Begin shrinking the pool based on the amount of detail
- Repeat the process of adding detail until we have three big ideas remaining

#### What next?
Provide me with a link to a Google Doc that includes your team members, anticipated development role(s), and a summary of your initial ideas.

#### What if someone is absent?
Absent students will need to join an existing group this week and may be placed to ensure that team sizes are appropriate.

### 11:30-1:00 - Break
Lunch break.

Begin working on research and prototyping.

## Homework

### Begin Research
- __Create a shared online document for this research and include me as a member__ (ssheffield@mica.edu). Google Doc, [Miro](https://miro.com/) board, etc. _Choose only one._
- __Each team member is responsible for contributing 4 pieces of research related to your team's initial idea__. Provide a small description of how you feel each item might be relevant or informative to some aspect of your game.
- Research can happen entirely online, but you might benefit from playing some games or spending time in the library as well.
- It's okay if there are duplicate references from different team members.
- __No more than 2 video game references per person.__

### Begin Prototyping
- __Everyone prototypes this week, regardless of your anticipated role on the team.__
- Each team member is responsible for creating a _very small_ prototype related to the initial ideas for your game. These should be playable enough to discuss and share next week.
- The prototype can be digital (2D or 3D), or physical (paper prototype, other materials, Kojima's LEGO).
- Please understand that we are currently just in the exploratory phase of development. Do not be afraid of making mistakes or coming up with something _too weird_ at this point!
