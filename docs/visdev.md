## What is Visual Development for Games?
Visual Development is the process of establishing the visual identity, style, and aesthetic direction of your game. It is not focused on producing finalized art for your game.  

This includes:
- **Concept Art**: Exploratory illustrations that establish mood, atmosphere, and visual tone
- **Key Art**: Signature images that capture the essence of your game's visual identity
- **Character Design**: Visual exploration of character appearance, silhouette, and personality
- **Prop Design**: Design of important objects, weapons, items, or environmental elements
- **Environment Design**: Exploration of settings, locations, and world-building

Visual development in games shares similarities with pre-visualization in film and animation. Both processes help creative teams align on a shared vision before committing resources to production. 

The goal at this stage is **exploration and iteration**, not finished assets. You're answering questions like: What does this world look like? How do characters fit into it? What's the visual tone?
