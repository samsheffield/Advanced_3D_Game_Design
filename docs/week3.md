### Begin Visual Development

**If you have enough information to proceed** (promising prototype(s), clear themes, characters, setting, etc.):

**If you feel uncertain about your game's direction**
- Focus on prototyping or develop agreed upon aspects of your game.

#### What is a Paint-over?
A paint-over (also sometimes referred to as kitbashing) is a technique where you:
1. Take a screenshot from your prototype or reference image
2. Import it into digital painting software
3. Paint directly over the image on new layers to explore visual ideas quickly

Paint-overs can be faster than creating finished illustrations from scratch and help you make informed decisions about your game's visual direction before production ramps up.
